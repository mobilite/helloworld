How this value is worked out
  • Only completed sales are used, never asking prices.
  • A sale counts only if it's the same card in the same condition or grade as yours. Lots, other languages, best-offer sales with hidden prices and sales over 6 months old are left out.
  • Each price includes the postage the buyer paid, converted to NZ$ using the rates in Fees & rates.
  • Recent sales count more: a sale from 30 days ago counts half as much as one from today.
  • With 5 or more sales, prices far outside the normal range are left out as unusual.
  • The value is the middle (median) price. The range shows where the middle half of sales landed.
  • With fewer than 3 matching sales, no value is given.
  • Tick or untick any sale to override these rules. Your choice is marked.
